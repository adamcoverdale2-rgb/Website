Gas, servicing & hire

Cylinder & equipment servicing

Scuba and air rifle cylinder testing, regulator and BCD servicing, and dry suit repairs.

  • Air & gas fills
  • Servicing
  • Equipment hire

We service equipment from a wide range of manufacturers through our partner dive centre. We focus on quality over quantity and take pride in our work, and our team can have you back diving in no time.

Manufacturers' recommendations vary, but it's generally accepted that equipment should be serviced every 100 dives or once a year, whichever comes first. If something doesn't feel right, we'll happily give your kit a health check.

Arrange a service

Cylinder service intervals

  • Oxygen cleaning: every 15 months
  • Visual inspection: every 30 months (2.5 years)
  • Hydrostatic test: every 60 months (5 years)
  • Every cylinder service includes a valve service
