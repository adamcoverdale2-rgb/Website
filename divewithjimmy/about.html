About us

Military attention to detail. A genuine love of the water.

Founded on a passion for all things underwater, Dive With Jimmy was created to help divers reach their full potential.

Jimmy Soos Founder & instructor

Jimmy learned to dive with the British Army in Cyprus, then kept diving through postings in the UK, Brunei and back to Cyprus. After leaving the Army he founded Dive With Jimmy to give every diver the training he wished he'd had. He loves wreck diving.

  1. 2019 BSAC Open Water Instructor
  2. Tech Twinset & accelerated decompression instructor; dived the Zenobia
  3. 2022 Closed circuit rebreather, then CCR MOD 2
  4. 2023 Cruise director on Pacific Master in Truk Lagoon and Bikini Atoll
  5. Today Qualified to 100m on open and closed circuit
jimmy@divewithjimmy.com

Emma Soos Snorkel, Explorers Club & Blue Oceans

Emma is an advocate for women in scuba diving and for looking after the ocean. She leads our SSI Snorkel Diver courses and the SSI Explorers Club, and heads up our work as an SSI Blue Oceans partner.

emma@divewithjimmy.com
