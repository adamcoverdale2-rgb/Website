Gas, servicing & hire

Air & gas fills

Breathing air, nitrox up to 100%, trimix and argon, plus HPA and PCP air rifle fills while you wait.

  • Air & gas fills
  • Servicing
  • Equipment hire

Our filling and blending facility runs on an L&W 450 compressor with a 4-whip filling panel and a 300 bar whip, filling 5 cylinders at 450 litres per minute. With our NRC booster pump we can supply breathing air, Enriched Air Nitrox up to 100%, trimix blends and argon for suit inflation.

Every fill is checked for quality and purity. You must show a certification for the gas you're asking for. Not qualified for nitrox or trimix yet? We can teach you.

Before leaving, please analyse your mix and record it in the gas log. Any discrepancies must be raised at the point of sale. Prices may change without notice.

Only pay for the gas you get

Oxygen, helium and argon are charged by the litre we put in, plus an air top-up if needed. If your cylinder arrives with 100 bar of oxygen and we fill to 230 bar, you only pay for 130 bar.
