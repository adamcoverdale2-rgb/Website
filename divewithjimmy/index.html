About us

Military attention to detail. A genuine love of the water.

Jimmy learned to dive with the British Army in Cyprus. Today he's qualified to 100m on open and closed circuit, and has run trips to Truk Lagoon and Bikini Atoll. With Emma leading our snorkel and Blue Oceans work, we'll look after you from your first lesson.
