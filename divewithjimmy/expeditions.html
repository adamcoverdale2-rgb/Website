Expeditions

Worldwide recreational and technical diving expeditions to some of the world's best destinations, with no hidden fees.

Our mission is to take divers beyond the ordinary, with underwater adventures that combine exploration, discovery and a shared passion for the ocean. From reefs and historic shipwrecks to challenging technical expeditions, we bring like-minded divers together in small groups with a personal approach.

Every trip is more than a diving holiday: a chance to discover new places, develop your skills, make lasting friendships and create memories that stay with you long after you've surfaced.

Every package includes

  • Cylinder rental
  • Breathing gases, including helium
  • Sofnolime for rebreathers
  • Boat fees
  • Guiding on the dives
  • Our first-hand knowledge of each destination
