SSI Technical: rebreather

SSI CCR Extended Range

Decompression rebreather diving on a trimix diluent.

Building on CCR Diving, this programme increases depth and duration, introduces decompression and adds more complex skills. Training is on the AP Inspiration, using trimix as the breathing gas to reduce gas density and improve safety.

Hire kit is available, but for the most benefit we recommend configuring your own.

What's included

All learning materials, instructor time and breathing gases (for student and instructor), and 5 days of open water training. Extra days are chargeable.

Extra costs: Open water site entry.

Price

£800 – £1,250
