BSAC Technical: open circuit

BSAC Combined Sports & Explorer Mixed Gas

Trimix diving to 50m, then 60m with a travel gas, in one course.

Theory, confined water and build-up dives, learning to use a travel gas to support diving with normoxic trimix, finishing with a qualifying 60m dive.

When you qualify you'll be able to plan and dive on trimix to 60m using a twinset and two stage cylinders.

You should be proficient at deeper diving, with 30 or more logged dives between 30m and 50m, and have dived to 30m or deeper in the 4 weeks before the course.

What's included

All learning materials, instructor time and breathing gases (for student and instructor).

Extra costs: Open water site entry and/or boat fees.
