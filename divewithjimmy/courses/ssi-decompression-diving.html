SSI Specialty courses

SSI Decompression Diving

Dive to 40 metres with limited decompression, up to 15 minutes.

Explore deeper sites and larger wrecks without the limits of traditional no-decompression diving. You'll use your recreational kit with a single decompression cylinder, and learn your computer's advanced features: gas switching, gas integration, time-to-reserve and time-to-surface.

Take advantage of our Decompression Diving bundle, which includes SSI Deep Diving and Enriched Air Nitrox.

What's included

Learning materials, equipment hire, instructor time and breathing gases (for student and instructor), and 2 days of open water training. Extra days are chargeable.

Extra costs: Open water site entry.

Price

£550 (bundle £800)
