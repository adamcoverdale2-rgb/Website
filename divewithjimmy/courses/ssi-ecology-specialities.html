SSI Specialty courses

SSI Ecology Specialties

Learn more about your favourite underwater animal or ecosystem.

There are so many ecology specialties to choose from, there's something for everyone. Pick one, or four! They can all be completed online at any time, and the open water sessions are optional.

Ecology specialties are open to qualified snorkel divers and scuba divers.

SSI ecology programmes include

  • Marine Ecology
  • Fish Identification
  • Coral Identification
  • Shark Ecology
  • Sea Turtle Ecology
  • Marine Mammal Ecology
  • Manta & Ray Ecology
  • Nudibranch Ecology
  • Marine Invertebrate Ecology
  • Freshwater Ecology
  • Blue Oceans
