SSI Professional

SSI Assistant Instructor

Start teaching, as part of SSI's Instructor Training Course.

As an SSI Assistant Instructor you can

  • Teach Try Scuba, Scuba Skills Update and Perfect Buoyancy
  • Teach Open Water Diver academic and pool/confined water sessions
  • Complete online upgrades for Computer Diving, Marine Ecology and SSI Explorer Specialty Instructor
  • Qualify as a Specialty Instructor in some courses
  • Go on to complete the Instructor Training Course

To complete the course

  • Complete sections 1–6 of the Instructor Training Course digital learning, including reviews and assessments
  • Pass all requirements and evaluations for Practical Application Sessions 1–7
  • Pass the Assistant Instructor final exam

What's included

All learning materials, instructor time and breathing gases (for student and instructor).

Extra costs: Open water site entry.
