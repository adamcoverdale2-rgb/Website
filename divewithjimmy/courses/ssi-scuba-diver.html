SSI Learn to scuba

SSI Scuba Diver

The perfect foundation to become a confident, safe diver.

You'll learn everything you need to scuba dive in open water to 12 metres with an SSI professional. The course is made up of half the elements of the full Open Water Diver course.

We recommend it for 10 to 15 year olds. At 16 we can upgrade you to Open Water Diver easily: you just complete the remaining academic and confined water sessions, plus two open water training dives.

It can also be combined with the SSI Dry Suit Diving course if you'd like to stay warmer on your dives.

What's included

All learning materials, equipment hire, instructor time and breathing gases (for student and instructor).

Extra costs: Open water site entry.
