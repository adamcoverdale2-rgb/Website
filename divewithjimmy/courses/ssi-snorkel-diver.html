SSI Snorkel & freediving

SSI Snorkel Diver

For anyone with a passion to discover the underwater world.

You'll learn to snorkel safely, choose and use your equipment, and master the skills while building your confidence. Led by Emma.

You don't need to be a strong swimmer and there's no minimum age. You just need to be comfortable in the water, able to keep yourself afloat, and ready for the adventure of a lifetime.

We run this course during our pool time on Tuesday evenings.

What's included

All equipment hire and instructor time.
