SSI Technical: rebreather

SSI CCR Diving (AP Inspiration)

No-decompression rebreather diving to 30 metres on the AP Inspiration.

This course introduces self-sufficiency and redundancy on a closed circuit rebreather. Become completely self-sufficient with SSI's training techniques, and learn rebreather dive planning, in-water skills, problem solving and emergency management.

You'll get a thorough grounding in pre-dive checks, assembly and how to deal with potential failures.

What's included

All learning materials, instructor time and breathing gases (for student and instructor), and 4 days of open water training. Extra days are chargeable.

Extra costs: Open water site entry.
