SSI Specialty courses

SSI DPV Diving

Whizz around underwater on a scooter and cover more ground on every dive.

Learn to plan and run dives with a diver propulsion vehicle (DPV), keep in touch with your buddy, and stay in control of your equipment. This course qualifies you to dive with a DPV to 30 metres.

What's included

Learning materials, equipment hire, instructor time and breathing gases (for student and instructor), and 1 day of open water training. Extra days are chargeable.

Extra costs: Open water site entry.
