SSI Technical: open circuit

SSI Extended Range Nitrox

A thorough introduction to decompression diving with nitrox.

A stand-alone programme or a stepping stone to Extended Range Trimix, teaching the foundation skills for all technical programmes.

Using a twinset, you'll plan and carry out limited decompression dives to 40 metres, and learn to use gases up to 100% oxygen for decompression. Can be completed on a single cylinder or twinset.

Hire kit is available, but for the most benefit we recommend configuring your own.

What's included

Equipment, pool entry, instructor time and breathing gases (for student and instructor), and 4 days of open water training. Extra days are chargeable.

Extra costs: Open water site entry.
