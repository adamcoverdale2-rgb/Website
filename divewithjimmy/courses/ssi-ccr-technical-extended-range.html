SSI Technical: rebreather

SSI XR CCR Technical Extended Range (60m Trimix)

Unlimited decompression rebreather dives to 60 metres.

Takes the skills of an extended range rebreather diver and adds more complex scenarios for safer diving at greater depths and durations. Training is on the AP Inspiration, using a trimix diluent and up to three bailout cylinders.

Hire kit is available, but for the most benefit we recommend configuring your own.

What's included

All learning materials, instructor time and breathing gases (for student and instructor), and 4 days of open water training. Extra days are chargeable.

Extra costs: Open water site entry and overnight accommodation.
