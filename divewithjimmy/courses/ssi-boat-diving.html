SSI Boat Diving

Reach the dive sites you can only get to by boat.

This course takes you away from the shore and gives you the skills and confidence to dive from a boat, from small craft to week-long liveaboards in remote places.

What's included

Learning materials, equipment hire, instructor time and breathing gases (for student and instructor).
