SSI Professional

SSI Specialty Instructor

Teach the specialties you love.

There are three routes to becoming a Specialty Instructor: complete the Specialty Instructor Seminar for the course; co-teach at least one complete course and have 10 logged dives in that specialty; or apply with proof of at least 20 logged dives in the specialty (or proof of experience for non-diving specialties).

Each Specialty Instructor course is £275, including use of the dive centre, instructor time and breathing gases where needed.

We can qualify you to teach

  • Deep Diving
  • Dry Suit Diving
  • Enriched Air Nitrox
  • Fish Identification
  • Independent Diving
  • Marine Ecology
  • Navigation
  • Night & Limited Visibility
  • Perfect Buoyancy
  • Photo & Video
  • Science of Diving
  • DPV
  • Search & Recovery
  • Shark Ecology
  • Diver Stress & Rescue
  • Wreck Diving
  • React Right
