SSI Specialty courses

SSI Diver Stress & Rescue

Learn to prevent accidents, carry out rescues and give emergency care.

This course helps you protect yourself and other divers. It's one of the most important skill sets you can have.

Combine it with SSI React Right to be able to carry out a rescue and give the right first aid.

What's included

Learning materials, equipment hire, instructor time and breathing gases (for student and instructor), and 2 days of open water training. Extra days are chargeable.

Extra costs: Open water site entry.
