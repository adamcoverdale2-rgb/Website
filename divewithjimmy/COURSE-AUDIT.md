# Course audit: divewithjimmy.com vs SSI and BSAC

Checked 25 November 2026. Every course page on divewithjimmy.com (from its sitemap) was compared with the matching course page on divessi.com or bsac.com: minimum age, prerequisites, logged dives, sessions and maximum depth.

**Result:** 50 courses listed. Most match the agency standards. Below are the corrections made on the new site, the differences left as they are, and problems with the current site and its booking links.

Please check the items marked **Confirm** before going live.

## 1. Corrected on the new site

| Course | Current site says | New site says | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| SSI Navigation | Minimum age **1** | Minimum age **10** | SSI: 10. Typo. |
| SSI Extended Range Nitrox | Prerequisite: Deep Diving **or** Nitrox 40 | Deep Diving **and** Enriched Air Nitrox 40 | SSI lists both, plus 24 logged dives. **Confirm** |

## 3. Problems with the current site

- **Two courses share one Square checkout link.** Computer Diving and Decompression Diving both link to the same product. Someone buying Decompression Diving (£550) may be charged for Computer Diving (£180). On the new site, Decompression Diving opens the enquiry form until it has its own link.
- **Both BSAC courses share one Square checkout link.** Twinset & ADP (£600) and Sports & Explorer Mixed Gas (£1,250–£1,500) go to the same product. Both open the enquiry form on the new site.
- **No checkout link:** Snorkel Diver (£75), Ecology Specialties (no price) and Specialty Instructor (£275). These open the enquiry form.
- **BSAC Diver Training Days page is broken** (it shows "Page not found") but it's in the menu. The new site has a short holding page. **Add a description and price.**
- **Ecology Specialties** has no price and no list of courses. The new page lists SSI's own ecology programmes. **Confirm** which you teach, and add a price.
- **Pool nights** say "book through the SSI calendar on the home page", but there's no calendar link on the site. The new "Book a pool night" button goes to the Square shop.
- **Public leftovers:** /test-page/ (a copy of the servicing page) and an empty /cylinder-fills/ page are live and in the sitemap.
- **Phone link:** the number shown is 07538 051564, but tapping it dials a US number (425-334-5956).

All 45 Square checkout links that do exist were tested and load correctly.
