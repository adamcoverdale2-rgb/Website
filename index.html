Welcome aboard

Small groups. Big dives.
Friendly people.

Scuba Murcia runs daily diving for every level, from shore dives and boat dives to deep wrecks. We teach in English, Spanish and Dutch, and our instructors keep groups small so you get time underwater, not time waiting.

We're a short ride from Cabo de Palos and the Islas Hormigas, one of Europe's best-protected marine reserves. Expect groupers, barracuda, eagle rays, octopus and walls covered in colour.

Technical diving

Go further.
Go deeper.

We teach TDI, PSAI and SSI technical courses and run technical dives, specialising in back-mounted twinset and sidemount. Our local playground includes deep wrecks and one of Europe's only warm-water cenote cave dives.

Talk to a tech instructor
  • 01

    Sidemount

    Streamlined, flexible configuration for caves, wrecks and travel.

  • 02

    Twinset & decompression

    Back-mounted doubles, gas planning and staged decompression.

  • 03

    Deep wrecks

    Historic steamships off Cabo de Palos at technical depths.

  • 04

    Cueva del Agua

    A geothermal cenote with warm, clear water all year round.

Where we dive

World-class sites on our doorstep

Cabo de Palos and the Islas Hormigas have been a protected marine reserve since 1995, and it shows. Here are a few of our favourites.

The jewel of the reserve. Big groupers, barracuda schools and sunfish in season.
