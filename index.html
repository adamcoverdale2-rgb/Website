Where we dive

Some of the best sites in the Med

The Islas Hormigas marine reserve was set up in 1995, and its marine life now thrives. Here are a few of our favourites.

Max 60 m+

Bajo de Fuera

The most protected spot in the reserve. Huge schools of amberjack, dentex, giant groupers, eagle rays, and three wrecks scattered across the reef.

Advanced with experience
40 m+

Islas Hormigas

Seven reserve sites, with reefs rising from the depths to just below the surface.

Advanced
28–42 m

Naranjito wreck

Sunk in a 1946 storm carrying oranges. Intact and upright, with barracuda and the odd sunfish.

Advanced
45 m

Carbonero wreck

An 88 m coal ship torpedoed in 1917. Upright, with plenty of room to penetrate.

Deep / technical
44–65 m

SS Stanfield

A 120 m Greek cargo ship lost in 1916, almost completely intact on the sand.

Trimix
29 °C

Cueva del Agua

A rare warm-water cenote, 45 minutes from the centre, with crystal-clear water.

Cave qualified
Max 14 m

Grand Canyon

A shore-dive maze of channels and gorges ending in a cavern streaming with light.

Open Water
Max 12 m

Cala Fría

Beneath the Cabo de Palos lighthouse: octopus, stingrays and swim-throughs.

Open Water

We also dive Cala Reona, Cala Escalera, Cala Cortina, the CBA tug wreck and the Cartagena marine reserve.

Why Scuba Murcia

Not just another dive centre

  • Professional team

    Full-time, multi-agency professionals with experience from recreational teaching to mixed gas and cave diving.

  • High-quality training

    Small classes and flexible schedules. We focus on buoyancy, breathing and finning, so courses are never box-ticking.

  • Personal service

    No automated booking system and no one-size-fits-all dive plan. Every enquiry is answered by a real person.

  • Environmentally aware

    A strict no-touch, no-harassment policy. We take part in local clean-up dives and environmental initiatives.

  • Top-quality kit

    Authorised dealers for Apeks, Aqualung, Fourth Element and XDeep.

  • Licensed & insured

    Registered in the Murcia region. We can arrange DAN dive insurance if you don't already have cover.
